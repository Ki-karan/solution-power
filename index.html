How Solar Works

Sunlight

Solar panels absorb sunlight, generating DC electricity

Solar Panels

Panels convert sunlight to DC power and send it to the inverter

Inverter

Converts DC to AC electricity for use in your home or business

Battery Storage

Excess energy stored in lithium batteries for night or cloudy days
